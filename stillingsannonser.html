Graduate Engineer Development Scheme 2024

Do you want to join the team who makes energy transition possible?

16.09.2023

  Stavanger

Graduate

If you are passionate about making a positive impact on securing the world's energy future, we invite you to join Subsea7’s industry-leading Graduate Engineer Development Scheme (GEDS) and contribute to our mission of shaping a sustainable tomorrow.

The Graduate Engineer Development Scheme
The Graduate Development Scheme allows the graduates to gain a variety of experiences, which include formal training sessions and on-the-job development, spanning from detailed design engineering to opportunities to work offshore.

Subsea7 provides an environment that promotes and supports the development of both business skills and technical knowledge, which enables our graduates to work effectively within our global organization.

As a graduate, you will become a valuable member of the team and collaborate across disciplines.

Our commitment to you:

  • Support from both our local and corporate offices
  • Opportunities to develop your technical and non-technical skills by attending courses, through field trips, offshore experience and in your day-to-day job
  • The chance to network with experts and the global Subsea7 community
  • To be an employer that cares about you and your personal development and welfare.
  • An extensive Sports & Social club with tailored activities and events
Qualifications:
Our engineering graduates typically have an engineering degree within one of the following areas: Mechanical, Civil, Geotechnics, Structural, Marine/Naval Technology, Material Technology, Power distribution, Offshore Technology, Energy and Environmental Engineering or Applied Physics and Mathematics.

What we want from our people:
The energy industry is constantly evolving, and we expect our graduates to be adaptable, quick learners, and open to embracing new technologies. We are looking for graduates who live our values: Safety, Integrity, Sustainability, Performance, Collaboration and Innovation.

The positions are based in Stavanger with onboarding in September 2024.

GEDS is just the beginning - your professional and personal development journey continues throughout your career at Subsea7.

Graduate 2024 - Structural & Marine Engineer

To create a more sustainable future, we need smart minds who can contribute to solving both these challenges!

23.09.2023

  Stavanger

Graduate

We are one of the leading oil and gas companies in Norway founded on more than 50 years of operations on the NCS. The world today is dependent on secure and stable access to energy, at the same time as the climate crisis needs solving. We will reduce our environmental impact, while at the same time supplying Europe with energy for a long time ahead. To create a more sustainable future, we need smart minds who can contribute to solving both these challenges.

We are skilled and engaged employees, working on platforms and in our offices in Hammerfest, Oslo and Stavanger. Everyone has important jobs with meaning. Vår Energi is moving at a full speed into the future, and we need young people with new impulses to face tomorrow’s challenges. Is that you?

What to expect as a graduate within Structural & Marine Engineering?
As a graduate, you will be enrolled in Vår Energi’s graduate program and work with task such as:

  • Perform fundamental technical evaluations with frequent guidance and mentoring
  • Interact with contractors and vendors
  • Lead minor studies with frequent guidance and mentoring
Qualifications
  • Recently graduated or graduating in 2024 with a minimum of MSc within Engineering
  • 0-2 years of working experience
  • Fluent written and spoken communication skills in English
Who are we looking for?
We expect you to be forward thinking, positive, and open for acquiring new skills. You are strategic and structured, curious and a problem solver. You are a team player with the motivation and skills to work across disciplines, motivated by continuous learning and development.

We put safety first in everything we do, and we expect you to do the same.

What you get
  • Safe and healthy work environment
  • A company that is large enough for you to work and develop within different professional fields, but yet small enough that we know and take care of each other
  • Solution-oriented work environment where we are not afraid to challenge ourselves and our partners for action
  • A diverse community with free identity- and culture expression that respects every individual’s dignity and recognizes the power of differences
  • Competitive benefits such as pension and insurance schemes, loan arrangements, share saving program, additional days off as well as remote working and flexible arrangements
